Executing this action will perform the following operations in the backend:
  • ☁️ Push to Domo Webform / DataSet: Uploads and synchronizes all candidate records directly to your Domo cloud dataset.
  • 📝 Cook Official CSV & Excel Ledger: Writes screening_results.csv with Date column (Col 1), final scores, and full Google Drive resume links (Col 23).
  • 📁 Google Drive Organization: Moves all candidate resumes into Selected/ and Rejected/ folders.
  • ✉️ Employee Emails: Dispatches interview availability requests with attached Question Kits to assigned Technical Interviewers.
  • 📩 HR Ultimate List: Sends executive summary digest email with the newly cooked CSV ledger attached to HR.
⚠️ Before clicking final dispatch, verify that you have assigned technical interviewers to all shortlisted candidates.
